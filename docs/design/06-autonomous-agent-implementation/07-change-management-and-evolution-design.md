## 4. The Backward-Compatibility Obligation, and the Boundary Against `05-api-contract-design.md`

Specification §5 fixes the platform-wide obligation: every change preserves every prior guarantee to an existing builder or built artifact, or breaks it only along the managed path §5 below realizes; this is INV-09 stated as a duty on every evolution, and it is the obligation `05-api-contract-design.md` §5 and `02-data-model-and-entity-design.md` §7–§8 each elaborate for their own slice. **This document's own contribution is not a fourth determination mechanism** — it is the composition that confirms every change, of whatever shape, is checked against exactly one of three already-existing determinations, and never zero.

### 4.1 The Composition, Stated as a Table

| # | Change Shape | Backward-Compatibility Determination Owned By | Composes At |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A change to the platform-primitive or built-application contract, either tier | `05-api-contract-design.md` §5 — diff-based for the platform-primitive tier, compositional (reading `02-data-model-and-entity-design.md` §7's own determination) for the built-application tier | `01-agent-runtime-and-control-design.md` §4.1's classification (charter §7 row 8), reading that determination |
| 2 | A change to a builder-defined or platform-owned schema | `02-data-model-and-entity-design.md` §7 — the Validation Engine's `backward_compatible_with_prior` determination, cited and never re-derived | The identical row, reading that determination |
| 3 | A change to any other prior guarantee — an invariant, a quality floor, or a published behavior not elaborated by either of the above | The general INV-09 check (`01-invariant-enforcement-design.md`, cited per the specification's own citation, never designed here) | The identical row, reading that check's own finding; deny-by-default where no elaboration and no established finding exists |

**This is the boundary drawn most carefully.** `05-api-contract-design.md` governs **the published contract** — its versioning identifier, its deprecation marking and withdrawal mechanics, its breaking-change diff, its sign-off point, and its detection-as-release-gate, for both tiers, in full. This document governs **the platform's evolution more broadly** — the obligation that every change, whether or not it touches a contract at all, is checked against whichever of the three rows above owns its shape, and that a change whose shape matches none of them is never silently treated as compatible. Neither document re-derives the other's determination: this document does not run a second diff over a contract change, and `05-api-contract-design.md` does not extend its own mechanism to a change that carries no contract or schema shape at all — that residual category is exactly what row three above names, composing with the general INV-09 check rather than a mechanism either document invents for it.

### 4.2 The Residual Gap Persists, Named Rather Than Closed

`05-api-contract-design.md` §5.3 states its own honest limit: a change whose contract shape is unchanged but whose returned meaning has materially shifted "is invisible to both mechanisms" it builds, and "this residual gap is not closed anywhere in this document." **This document does not close it either, at the more general level it governs.** A change to a published behavior that carries no contract shape, no schema shape, and no invariant this library's own checks can register is not caught by row three of §4.1 above any more than it is caught by `05-api-contract-design.md`'s own diff — because row three's own general INV-09 check reads whichever elaboration already exists, and a behavioral shift with no elaboration to read leaves nothing for a deny-by-default posture to act on except the absence itself. This document states the limit plainly rather than claiming a broader mandate closes what a narrower one already disclosed it could not.

## 5. Deprecation and Migration — Composed From Two Already-Built Paths, Never a Third

Specification §6 requires that a change retiring, replacing, or moving a prior guarantee follow a managed, announced, time-bounded, reversible path, and that this document own "the general scheduling and communication of that managed path across every kind of change" while the contract-specific version rules remain `05-api-contract-design.md`'s.

### 5.1 Every Capability's Own Retirement Is Already a Contract or a Schema Event

On this platform's own architecture, a capability has no reach independent of a contract surface or a schema: `05-api-contract-design.md` §3.2 realizes every builder- or extender-reachable operation as a tagged OpenAPI operation — the concrete tagging mechanism `03-architecture-realization-design.md` §5.1 hands to that document by name — and `01-business-and-ux/03-platform-capability-model.md`'s own capability set is realized entirely through the platform-primitive or built-application contract tiers `05-api-contract-design.md` §2 fixes, or through a builder-defined schema `02-data-model-and-entity-design.md` owns. **There is no third, freestanding shape a capability's own deprecation could take that is not already one of these two.** A capability's retirement is therefore always discharged as an operation-level deprecation under `05-api-contract-design.md` §4.1 (the `deprecated` flag, the `Sunset` header, the concurrent-major-version mechanism) or a schema-level managed-path routing under `02-data-model-and-entity-design.md` §7 — never a parallel, capability-shaped mechanism this document invents alongside them.

### 5.2 What This Document's Own Obligation Actually Adds

The general scheduling obligation the specification hands this document is realized as a floor on every deprecation, regardless of which elaboration mechanism instantiates it: an announced start, a finite window fixed in advance (the specific duration owned by `03-software-and-architecture/06-non-functional-requirements.md`, keyed to and never re-derived), and a defined reversible path (INV-08, composing with `01-agent-runtime-and-control-design.md` §5.1's precondition 4). This floor is not enforced by a scheduling structure this document maintains — it is checked at the identical human-review-gate point (§8 below, row 2) that already requires a human decision before any deprecation, withdrawal, or retirement is adopted, so a deprecation lacking an announced, finite, reversible shape simply never clears the gate the review-trigger table already fixes for that row.

### 5.3 Migration and Dependency Re-Evaluation, Cited and Not Redesigned

A migration of committed data or a schema follows `02-data-model-and-entity-design.md` §7–§8's own safety constraints in full; a failed or reverted evolution recovers along `06-self-correction-and-fallback-design.md`'s ladder and `05-release-and-rollback-design.md`'s rollback (§11 below states this document's own composition with the former). A dependency change is re-evaluated for its license under `09-licensing-and-dependency-compliance-design.md`'s own policy; this document routes the change-management path a license re-evaluation travels and redefines none of the policy it must satisfy.

## 6. The Documentation-Update Requirement — a Merge-Gate Mechanism, Not an Exhortation

Specification §7 fixes that a change and the documentation of it are one unit, and that a change whose governing-document update has not been made, and verified for cross-document consistency, is incomplete. This document realizes both halves — the update's existence, and the update's verification — as two distinct floors on the identical enforcement point, never as separate mechanisms.

### 6.1 Where the Floor Sits

A change to platform-core source, a schema, a published contract, or a governing document that alters what a governing document records is not considered complete — and does not clear the Merge Gate `02-ci-cd-pipeline-design.md` already fixes — until (a) the corresponding update to every governing document it alters is present in the same change, and (b) that update has been verified self-consistent under §7 below. **This is not a new named check added to `02-ci-cd-pipeline-design.md` §5's closed list.** Exactly as `05-api-contract-design.md` §8.1 already establishes for breaking-change detection — "no new bullet, no new named check, and no amendment to `04-devops-and-cloud-infra/02-ci-cd-pipeline-spec.md` §5's closed list is required or proposed" — this document's own finding is classified directly into the identical Critical/High severity vocabulary `02-governance-and-security/02-security-policy.md` §6 already fixes, and consumed by that Merge Gate's own already-existing vulnerability bullet. For an autonomous agent-authored change, this composes at `01-agent-runtime-and-control-design.md` §9.2's own execution channel — the identical Merge Gate every platform-core change clears, "with no agent-specific bypass" — so this document introduces no agent-specific enforcement path either.

### 6.2 What Blocks, and the Honest Limit on Half of It

**A mechanical failure of (b) blocks outright.** Where §7's mechanical checks find a broken cross-reference or a quotation that does not match its source, the change is refused; there is no legitimate reason to approve a broken citation into the corpus, so this is never a `held-pending-approval` state — it is an ordinary `refused` outcome, corrected before the change may proceed.

**Whether (a) — the update's mere existence — was actually made is machine-verifiable only where a structural signal already exists for it.** A contract change already produces `05-api-contract-design.md`'s own `contract-version-lifecycle` event; a schema change already produces `02-data-model-and-entity-design.md`'s own version-catalog row. Where such a signal exists, its presence is the mechanical evidence that the corresponding update occurred. **Where a change alters a governing document's own prose — an ownership boundary, a rule's wording, a boundary statement — with no such structural signal, this document does not claim the update's presence is machine-checkable at all.** This is stated as an honest limit, not concealed: what this document's mechanism can and does catch is the update's **absence manifesting as a downstream symptom** — a stale cross-reference, an enumeration that no longer agrees with the mechanism it describes — which is exactly what §7's mechanical and flaggable checks are built to surface. A prose-only change whose omission produces no such symptom anywhere in the corpus is not caught by this document's mechanism, and this document does not claim otherwise.

## 7. The Documentation-Consistency Check — Mechanical, Flaggable, and Neither

The map's own definition, cited and realized rather than restated: **"a change-impact verification that, when one document changes, confirms related documents stay aligned and flags those needing update."** This project's own authoring history is a live record of exactly the drift class this mechanism must catch, produced under the same discipline this document now formalizes — read as evidence, per the ticket's own instruction, never as a set of instructions to satisfy.

### 7.1 What Is Mechanically Verifiable — Fully, Without Judgment

Three checks resolve to a definite yes-or-no, with no interpretive step between the check and its finding:

- **Cross-reference resolution.** A citation of the form "document `X`, §`N`" resolves to a yes-or-no fact: does document `X`'s current heading list carry a section numbered `N`? `BACKLOG.md` §1t records exactly this failure twice — a citation naming the wrong document entirely, and a citation naming the wrong section of the right document — and both are fully mechanical to detect: the check needs only the citing document's own citation and the target document's own current heading list, nothing about what either document *means*.
- **Verbatim-quotation matching.** Any string enclosed in quotation marks and attributed to a source is checked, character-for-character (normalized for whitespace), against that source's actual text. `BACKLOG.md` §1w's entire defect family — a fabricated quotation with no source anywhere, a truncated quotation presented as complete, a stitched quotation joining non-adjacent sentences, and a stitched phrase blending several real, adjacent sources into a string that exists verbatim in none of them — is caught by this single check without exception: none of the four variants produces an exact match against the cited source, because none of them *is* the cited source's text. This is the mechanism's strongest result, precisely because §1w's own closing observation names why it matters: **"a quotation mark is a claim of provenance,"** and a claim of provenance is the one part of a document's prose that a machine can verify against its own stated target without understanding a single word of what either document argues.
- **Self-referential section resolution.** A document's own internal `§N` citations, checked against its own final heading list, are the identical check as cross-reference resolution applied reflexively. `BACKLOG.md` §1w records this as "the only variant... fully mechanical to check" because "the document contradicts itself" — no external source is even needed.

### 7.2 What Is Flaggable — Detected, Never Auto-Resolved

One check surfaces a genuine finding that this mechanism does not, and must not, resolve on its own authority:

- **Enumeration and count agreement.** Where a document names, as a specific number or an enumerated list, how many steps a mechanism owned by another document carries, the check compares that number against the owning document's own current arity and surfaces a mismatch. **`BACKLOG.md` §1o is the concrete proof this finding must never auto-resolve to "stale, fix it."** A mechanism gained a fourth check; three sibling documents kept enumerating three; and one of those three-item enumerations was **correct in its own context**, because the fourth check's own precondition — an end-user actor reaching content through a construct — could not be met at that site. `BACKLOG.md` §1o's own words state the consequence precisely: *"this is not 'add the fourth check everywhere.' Each site is judged on whether the fourth check is applicable in its own context. A blanket correction would introduce a new error — asserting a check fires where its own precondition cannot be met."* A check that auto-corrected every three-item list to four, on the strength of the arity mismatch alone, would have introduced exactly that new error. **This document's own mechanism therefore treats an arity mismatch as a flag, never a fix**: it surfaces the mismatch, the mechanism whose arity changed, and every citing document affected, and routes the finding to the human-review gate (§8, row 6 below) rather than resolving it — because whether a narrower enumeration is stale or correctly context-bound is precisely the judgment `BACKLOG.md` §1o proves this mechanism cannot make.
- **Reverse-citation propagation.** A grep-style reverse search for every document citing the one that changed produces a candidate list of sites that *might* need an update. Producing the list is mechanical; determining which candidates genuinely need a change, versus which are unaffected by the specific thing that changed, is the same shape of judgment as the row above and is flagged identically, never auto-resolved.

### 7.3 What Is Neither Checkable Nor Flaggable — the Honest Limit

**Whether two documents still mean the same thing where their wording differs, with no quotation mark and no shared citation anchoring the comparison, is not reachable by this mechanism at all.** `BACKLOG.md` §1ab is the clearest evidence of this exact shape: a specification document's own citation pointed at a numeric value that was never actually present at the cited location, for four consecutive statements, and the gap was found only by a reader who happened to open the cited section and check its content directly — not by any structural signal the corpus itself carried. `BACKLOG.md` §1ab's own refinement states the general form: **"verify inherited citations too"** — but verifying an inherited citation is still, at bottom, a cross-reference-resolution or a content-presence check (§7.1's shape); it is not evidence that free-standing, unattributed paraphrase drift is checkable, only that *attributed* citations should never be trusted unread. A document's own unquoted prose that once accurately paraphrased another document, and has since gone stale because the other document changed with no citation linking the two, carries no structural marker this mechanism can grab onto — no quotation to diff, no `§N` to resolve, no enumerated count to compare. **This document does not claim to catch that case, states so plainly, and treats the claim otherwise as the single most misleading one this mechanism could make** — the honest-limit discipline `05-api-contract-design.md` §5.3 and `06-self-correction-and-fallback-design.md` §8 each already model, applied here to documentation consistency specifically.

### 7.4 Where the Check Runs, and What Each Finding Does

The check runs at the identical Merge-Gate point §6.1 already fixes, over the governing library the change touches. A §7.1 finding (a broken cross-reference, a mismatched quotation) is classified into the Critical/High vocabulary and blocks the merge outright — `refused`, corrected before the change proceeds, per §6.2 above. A §7.2 finding (an arity or propagation mismatch) is captured as `held-pending-approval` and routed to the human-review gate's row 6 (§8 below) — the change does not proceed autonomously until a human confirms whether the flagged site is stale or correctly narrower. A §7.3 case produces no finding at all, because the mechanism was never given a structural signal to evaluate.

## 10. Future / Not-Yet-Authorized Capabilities — Recording, Separation, and Promotion

`01-business-and-ux/02-prd.md` §4 fixes the category and its rules in full; C-22 (multi-language code export) and C-26 (runtime AI automation) are its two current members. ### 10.1 Recording — Already Discharged, Confirmed Rather Than Duplicated

### 10.2 Separation — Composed With `01-agent-runtime-and-control-design.md`'s Existing Scope Judgment, Never a New Gate

Specification-adjacent enforcement of the category's own prohibition — "must not be implemented, expanded, or relied upon" — composes with an already-built mechanism rather than a new one. `01-agent-runtime-and-control-design.md` §4.1 already builds a judgment-call determination for whether a proposed action's declared shape "pursues no non-goal of `01-business-and-ux/01-vision-and-charter.md` §4," recorded rather than assumed, with an unrecorded determination treated as a fail on the identical deny-by-default posture `01-invariant-enforcement-design.md` §5.2 already applies to INV-05's own judgment half. **This document's own finding is that the identical determination must additionally confirm the action's target is not, and does not depend on or expand, a capability presently held in the Future / Not-Yet-Authorized category** — a Future / Not-Yet-Authorized capability's own status, per `01-business-and-ux/02-prd.md` §4, is definitionally outside authorized scope until the sole authorization path (§10.3 below) is exercised, so an action targeting one fails precondition 2 of `01-agent-runtime-and-control-design.md` §5.1 by the identical mechanism that document already built, never a sixth precondition or a parallel gate this document constructs. ### 10.3 Promotion — a Spec-Phase Act This Document Flags, Never Performs

`01-business-and-ux/02-prd.md` §4 fixes that "the sole path out of this category is explicit authorization in a later revision of this document" — a revision of the **specification**. This document does not, and structurally cannot, author or trigger that revision: a design document never binds a specification document to change, the exact line `ADR-REGISTER.md` live issue 6 already records ADR-022 as having crossed. **Promotion is exclusively a spec-phase act.** This document's own mechanism plays no role in deciding whether or when a promotion occurs; its role begins only after one has — at that point, `01-business-and-ux/02-prd.md` §4's own text fixes that "the capability is placed into an active priority tier, its recorded dependencies and build-order position take effect, and the rules above cease to apply," which for the design phase means the newly active capability is scheduled a design document exactly as every other capability received one, following `implementation-document-map.md`'s own pyramid-ordering convention, with no special-cased fast path. **The promotion event itself, once it has occurred at the spec phase, is a change to what a governing document owns — `01-business-and-ux/02-prd.md`'s own capability table — and is therefore, on this document's own mechanism, itself a §8 row 6 candidate**, requiring the identical documentation-update-and-consistency check (`implementation-document-map.md` gaining a new scheduled row, the capability model's own enumeration of active capabilities changing) before the newly authorized capability's own design work may be treated as unblocked.
